# Carrier claims policy

Delivered-but-not-received: ask the customer to check with neighbors and household members for 48 hours after the delivery scan. If the parcel is still missing after 48 hours, open a carrier trace. UPS accepts claims for 60 days after shipment. FedEx accepts claims for 21 days. USPS accepts claims for 60 days.

Damaged parcels: ask the customer for photos of the box and the item. Damage claims need photos of the outer packaging. Do not ask the customer to return the damaged item for orders under $50.

Lost parcels: a parcel with no tracking scan for 7 calendar days is treated as lost. Open a carrier trace and offer the customer a resolution within 1 business day.
