# Refund and reship policy

Orders under $50: a refund or a reship may be offered immediately without a return. Choose the cheaper option unless the customer needs the item by a specific date.

Orders $50 to $250: reship is preferred when the customer wants the item. A refund is preferred when the customer no longer needs it. Any reship or refund above $50 needs human approval.

Orders above $250: always escalate to a human before offering anything.

Never offer a refund and a reship for the same order. Never promise delivery dates that the carrier has not confirmed.
